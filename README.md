# AtliQ Hardwares - Sales & Finance Data Analysis

This project provides an in-depth analysis of AtliQ Hardwares' sales performance and financial metrics across global markets, customers, and fiscal years. Built using Excel, Power Query, and Power Pivot, this repository contains interactive reports designed to support strategic decision-making, performance benchmarking, and target tracking.

---

## **Sales Reports**

### **Project Objectives**
1. **[Customer Performance Report](Customer%20Performance%20Report.pdf):** Tracks Net Sales across key global accounts (e.g., Amazon, AtliQ Exclusive, Flipkart) from FY2019 to FY2021, analyzing growth trends year-over-year.
2. **[Market Performance vs Target Report](Market%20Performance%20vs%20Target%20Report.pdf):** Evaluates country-level performance against defined sales targets to identify underperforming or high-growth regions.

### **Business Value & Key Insights**
* **Customer Growth:** Global accounts like Amazon grew from 12.2M INR in FY2019 to 82.1M INR in FY2021 (+218.9% growth vs FY20).
* **Regional Performance:** Overall net sales reached 598.9M INR in FY2021, missing aggregate targets by 54.9M INR (-9.17%).
* **Strategic Utility:** Enables business teams to design targeted customer discounts, optimize promotional pricing, strengthen negotiations, and prioritize market expansion efforts.

---

## **Finance Reports**

### **Project Objectives**
1. **[P&L Statement by Fiscal Year](P%26L%20Statement%20by%20Fiscal%20Year.pdf):** Highlights annual revenue, Cost of Goods Sold (COGS), and Gross Margin trends from FY2019 to FY2021.
2. **[P&L Statement by Months](P%26L%20Statement%20by%20Months.pdf):** Breaks down monthly sales, COGS, and Gross Margin percentages across fiscal quarters to spot seasonality.
3. **[P&L Statement by Markets](P%26L%20Statement%20by%20Markets.pdf):** Provides country-wise profitability metrics for FY2021.

### **Business Value & Key Insights**
* **Margin Trends:** Gross margin percentage adjusted from 41.4% in FY2019 down to 36.4% in FY2021 as total volume expanded rapidly.
* **Top Market Profitability:** In FY2021, top-performing profit drivers by market included the USA (32.5M USD GM, 37.0% GM%), India (51.6M USD GM, 32.0% GM%), and South Korea (17.6M USD GM, 35.9% GM%).
* **Strategic Utility:** Supports executive decision-making, financial forecasting, cost optimization, and stakeholder alignment.

## **Skills Applied**

### **Technical Skills**
* **ETL Pipeline:** Extracted, transformed, and cleaned raw data using Power Query.
* **Data Modeling:** Generated automated Date Tables and structured Star/Snowflake schema relationships using Power Pivot.
* **Date & Fiscal Logic:** Derived custom fiscal months and fiscal quarters tailored to business operations.
* **DAX & Calculations:** Created custom measures and calculated columns for net sales, growth rates, and margins.

### **Soft & Analytical Skills**
* **Financial Literacy:** Deep understanding of P&L structure, revenue drivers, COGS, and gross margin dynamics.
* **User-Centric Design:** Formatted visual layouts to maximize scannability for business leadership.
* **Structured Planning:** Devised systematic workflows for efficient report development and maintenance.
